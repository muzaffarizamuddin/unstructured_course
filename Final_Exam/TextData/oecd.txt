OECD lowers world GDP forecast to 3pc for 2022

ISTANBUL: The Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development (OECD) said on Wednesday that it has lowered its global economic growth forecast from 4.46 per cent to 3 per cent for this year.

Russia's war on Ukraine and China's lockdown measures have damaged the recovery, Anadolu Agency citing the OECD said in a report.

"Prior to the war, the world economy was on track for a strong, albeit uneven, recovery from COVID-19," it added.

The report's 2023 forecast was 2.8 per cent for global economic growth.

On inflation, it said: "The war in Ukraine has quashed hopes for a quick end to rising inflation from Covid-19 related supply bottlenecks seen across the global economy during 2021 and early 2022."

While the report lowered GDP forecasts for G-20, the US, UK, and the eurozone, it raised the forecast for Türkiye.

It is expected that G-20 countries' economies will grow by 2.9 per cent this year and 2.8 per cent in 2023, while the forecasts were 3.6 per cent and 0 per cent for the UK, respectively, and 2.5 per cent and 1.2 per cent for the US.

The eurozone economy is expected to expand by 2.6 per cent and 1.6 per cent in 2022 and 2023, respectively, it added.

The Turkish economy is also forecasted to expand by 3.7 per cent and 3 per cent over the same period.

Germany's GDP forecast for this year was 1.9 per cent, while 1.7 per cent for next year.

The inflation expectations rose for almost all countries, according to the report.
