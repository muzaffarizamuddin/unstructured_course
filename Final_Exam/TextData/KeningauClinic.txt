KENINGAU: A health clinic in Jalan Tulid, Sook, near here, was destroyed in a fire today.

A Sabah Fire and Rescue Department operations centre spokesman said the department received a call about the fire at 4.48pm.

Eight personnel from the Sook Fire and Rescue Station were despatched to the scene.


"When the team arrived, most of the clinic was completely burnt as it was built with wood.

"The team brought the fire under control at about 6pm."
