For early detection and screening.

PANTAI Hospital Cheras has launched a health screening centre to encourage early detection of medical problems.
As reported in the 2019 National Health and Morbidity Survey, non-communicable diseases (NCDs) contributed to 67 per cent of premature deaths in Malaysia.
NCDs such as diabetes, high blood pressure, cardiovascular complications and others have overtaken infectious diseases as the number one cause of death in Malaysia.
"It is important for everyone to be aware of their health status. Health screening helps us find out if we have any underlying disease or condition or if we are at high risk. It is an effective way to detect a particular condition or disease early, even when there are no signs or symptoms," says Pantai Hospital Cheras CEO, Rachel Yew.
Early detection means getting the right treatment at the right time, which gives us better control over our health, she adds.
