KOTA BARU: Dengue has so far claimed two lives in Kelantan as of June 11 this year.

Kelantan health director Datuk Dr Zaini Hussi said no dengue fatalities were recorded in the same period last year.

He said Kelantan has recorded a total of 197 dengue cases as of June 11, compared to 101 cases over the corresponding period the year prior.

"There was a 96 case increase or about 95.05 per cent between January to June 11 this year compared to last year.

Dr Zaini said Kelantan has five dengue outbreak localities, with all of them being in the Kota Baru district. The five areas are Badang, Panji, Kota, Taman Sri Mahang and Bukit China 2.

He said more than 90,000 premises as well as residential homes have undergone inspection. Of the total, 2,967 of them were found to be Aedes mosquito breeding grounds.

He said the department has issued 461 compound notices amounting to RM230,500 to the offenders.

"During the same period, we also issued another 36 notices to house owners and others ordering them to clean up their compounds which have the potential to become Aedes breeding grounds."
